I am a **Ph.D. candidate at the University of Nevada, Reno**, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence systems, high-performance computing, and distributed machine learning.

My research focuses on building **efficient and scalable systems for modern AI workloads**, particularly in resource-efficient machine learning, distributed systems, GPU computing, and large language model inference and serving.

My recent work explores **adaptive LLM inference using opportunistic GPU resources in HPC environments**, efficient data pipelines for multi-job deep learning training, and scalable distributed systems for verifiable machine learning.

I am particularly interested in **AI infrastructure, ML systems, distributed computing, and performance optimization**, especially where systems research can improve scalability, resource utilization, and the efficiency of large-scale machine learning workloads.
